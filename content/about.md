## ♡ Hello!

I love drawing, game development, and music theory + composition. I also like console modding, 2000s/2010s era video games, and playing piano.

I specialise in drawing cute girls, and designing and developing gameplay systems. My works are primarily made with Clip Studio Paint EX and GameMaker.

My dream is to one day make an original game with characters and a world that people enjoy.
